\section{Policy Computation, Recorded Quantities, and Evaluation Design}
\label{supp:recording-overview}
This section states where each quantity is recorded (Table~\ref{tab:recording-roles}), which study compares it (Table~\ref{tab:cohorts}) and with what sampling unit and intervals. For C1, the shared directions are fitted to the action-expert state H after L1, L9 and L18 at the first query and first generation step of the LIBERO-Plus recordings (main Figs.~\ref{M-fig:teaser}a and~\ref{M-fig:model-measurement}).

\paragraph{Comparisons along the control loop.}
At every block, the action-expert tokens attend to that block's prefix keys and values, which are computed once per query; the image therefore enters the action expert at every layer and every generation step. The robot executes the first five commands of each plan and then replans from a new observation. Reconstruction asks how much of an observed response a fitted span retains; its information, mean and capacity controls are in Secs.~\ref{sec:information-budget} and~\ref{sec:mean-controls}. Command readouts compare predictive information (Sec.~\ref{sec:current-basis-readout}), while associations describe covariation on matched cases (Secs.~\ref{sec:plus-sites} and~\ref{sec:supp-bounded-internal}). Paired executions measure repeatability, motion and outcomes (Secs.~\ref{sec:supp-q0-consistency}, \ref{sec:supp-matched-motion} and~\ref{sec:five-arm}). The later-observation, exposure-timing and original-LIBERO studies extend these questions to other states and schedules (Secs.~\ref{sec:supp-dynamic-visual}, \ref{sec:window552} and~\ref{supp:legacy}).

\paragraph{Policies and evaluation design.}
The two policies generate plans of different length, $10\times32$ for $\pi_{0.5}$ and $50\times32$ for $\pi_0$, but both use ten generation steps and execute five controls before replanning. The $\pi_0$ paired executions and the separate $\pi_0$ 720-episode study use the same checkpoint; each has its own executions and interventions. The paired executions reuse the 268 settings of the LIBERO-Plus recordings. Their assignments were fixed before their outcomes were read, but after the results of the LIBERO-Plus recordings were known.

\paragraph{Hidden states come from the policy's own run; attention comes from a replay.}
Action-expert H is recorded along the policy's own generation; $P$ and $W$ describe attention recomputed at each branch's saved generation state $x_j$ and time. The policy's forward pass applies all 18 blocks through a rematerialized scan. The diagnostic pass applies the same frozen block parameters through a separate full-depth scan, retaining blocks 0, 8 and 17 (L1, L9 and L18). Saved generation states, times and visibility masks define its inputs. This distinction applies throughout the LIBERO-Plus analyses (Table~\ref{tab:recording-roles}). Final actions of the policy's own run are checked against the reference baseline before H is adopted; diagnostic readouts and velocities are checked exactly against in-graph controls. Section~\ref{sec:supp-protocol-card} compares diagnostic and policy hidden states and velocities; no such comparison exists for P or W.

The replay shows how the allocation P becomes the projected output W. It recomputes the prefix; normalized suffix states produce queries, keys and values. Rotary embeddings transform queries and keys, queries are scaled by the head dimension, and cached prefix keys and values are concatenated with the suffix's. Softmax spans all visible keys before value aggregation. The output projection produces $W$ before the adaptive attention gate and residual addition. The feed-forward sublayer and its gated residual then produce post-block H. Prefix V stays fixed within a query, while suffix V changes with the generation state.

\paragraph{Sampling units and intervals by study.}
Layer responses compare response size with each layer's clean scale. The comparison uses 1,199 of 1,200 internal pairs on 40 tasks; one headlight pair is missing. It reports all 200 task--condition means and five equal-task condition means at the first query, first generation step and first five plan positions, each response RMS divided by its own layer's clean RMS.

Camera calibration predicts a larger-angle response on the same task domain, contrasting 234 original cases with 240 separate start-state/run cases. Its full-minus-compact loss carries 99.375\% conditional intervals from 10,000 suite-stratified task draws, with an eight-effect adjustment and fixed predictions. Training tasks include the target angle.

The exploratory joint task/family reconstruction tests transfer when both the task and the family are held out, and retains all 12 family/layer cells. Background, Camera, Light and Sensor Noise contribute 34/32/40/39 task components and 118/112/152/154 cases; Light includes 18 records without a LIBERO-Plus difficulty level. Its 95\% task-component intervals use 2,000 draws, condition on the fitted directions and carry no multiplicity adjustment; each query keeps 160 coefficients across ten positions.

The common-task comparison places internal summaries and behavior on the same 28 tasks and 404 pairs. Hidden-state and attention summaries are descriptive means; commands and motion have pointwise 95\% task-block intervals; outcomes keep the four-family Bonferroni adjustment for simultaneous 95\% coverage. Motion ratios of task-equal means have no interval here; Sec.~\ref{sec:plus-motion} gives the paired motion differences.

The paired executions estimate the paired success change against two clean runs on the prespecified primary support of 28 tasks and 202 settings per policy, with fixed noise weights. All 536 policy--setting cases are archived; 39 run outcomes are unknown (25 technical failures and 14 that fail the analysis validation criteria) and enter bounds over missing outcomes. The 2,000 task-bootstrap draws give four-family-adjusted 98.75\% intervals for the success changes (Sec.~\ref{sec:five-arm}).

The exposure-window study separately compares four timing schedules. Its complete readout (138/138 start states, 552/552 episodes) uses 23 whole tasks, three noise draws per task and two fixed initial states. The prespecified endpoint for initial state 24 is the terminal world-XY distance from the target to the current goal center, $R_{30}-N_{30}$; initial state 25 repeats the fixed rule. Its 10,000 shared whole-task bootstrap draws and pointwise 95\% intervals describe this timing comparison (Sec.~\ref{sec:window552}).

\paragraph{Implementation notes.}
Masked attention logits are accumulated at higher precision and cast to the model precision before value aggregation. The exposure-window bootstrap uses seed 20261008.
