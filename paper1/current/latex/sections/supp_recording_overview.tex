\section{Policy Computation, Recorded Quantities, and Evaluation Design}
\label{supp:recording-overview}
This section states where each quantity is recorded (Table~\ref{tab:recording-roles}), which study compares it (Table~\ref{tab:cohorts}) and with what sampling unit and intervals. For C1, the shared directions are fitted to the action-expert state H after L1, L9 and L18, at the first query and first generation step of the LIBERO-Plus recordings (main Figs.~\ref{M-fig:teaser}a and~\ref{M-fig:model-measurement}).

\paragraph{Each study asks a different question.}
Reconstruction asks how much of an observed response fitted directions retain (information, mean and capacity controls: Secs.~\ref{sec:information-budget} and~\ref{sec:mean-controls}); command readouts, what the response predicts about the commands (Sec.~\ref{sec:current-basis-readout}); associations, how quantities covary on matched cases (Secs.~\ref{sec:plus-sites} and~\ref{sec:supp-bounded-internal}). Paired executions measure repeatability, motion and outcomes (Secs.~\ref{sec:supp-q0-consistency}, \ref{sec:supp-matched-motion} and~\ref{sec:five-arm}). The later-observation and original-LIBERO studies extend these questions to other states and schedules (Secs.~\ref{sec:supp-dynamic-visual} and~\ref{supp:legacy}).

\paragraph{Policies and evaluation design.}
The two policies generate plans of different length, $10\times32$ for $\pi_{0.5}$ and $50\times32$ for $\pi_0$, but both use ten generation steps and execute five controls before replanning. The paired executions reuse the 268 settings of the LIBERO-Plus recordings. Their assignments were fixed before their outcomes were read, but after the results of the LIBERO-Plus recordings were known.

\paragraph{Hidden states come from the policy's own run; attention comes from a replay.}
The action-expert states H are recorded during the policy's own generation, so they are what the policy computed. The attention quantities $P$ and $W$ are not stored in that pass; a diagnostic replay recomputes them from each branch's saved plan $x_j$ and generation time, with the same frozen weights. This holds throughout the LIBERO-Plus analyses (Table~\ref{tab:recording-roles}). Section~\ref{sec:supp-protocol-card} compares the hidden states and velocities of the replay with the policy's own; no such comparison exists for P or W.

\paragraph{Sampling units and intervals by study.}
The shared-direction reconstruction is exploratory; it holds out both task and family and reports all 12 family/layer cells. Its unit is the task component: tasks that share a clean scene, held out together. Background, Camera, Light and Sensor Noise contribute 34/32/40/39 task components and 118/112/152/154 cases; Light includes 18 records without a LIBERO-Plus difficulty level. Its 95\% intervals resample task components (2,000 draws), condition on the fitted directions and carry no multiplicity adjustment. Each query keeps 160 coefficients across ten positions.

The common-task comparison places internal summaries and behavior on the same 28 tasks and 404 pairs. Hidden-state and attention summaries are descriptive means. Commands and motion have pointwise 95\% task-block intervals; outcomes keep the four-family Bonferroni adjustment for simultaneous 95\% coverage. Motion ratios of task-equal means have no interval here; Sec.~\ref{sec:plus-motion} gives the paired motion differences.

The paired executions estimate the paired success change against two clean runs on the prespecified primary support of 28 tasks and 202 settings per policy, with fixed noise weights. All 536 policy--setting cases are archived; 39 run outcomes are unknown (25 technical failures and 14 that fail the analysis validation criteria) and enter bounds over missing outcomes. The 2,000 task-bootstrap draws give four-family-adjusted 98.75\% intervals for the success changes (Sec.~\ref{sec:five-arm}).

\paragraph{Implementation notes.}
The policy's forward pass applies all 18 blocks through a rematerialized scan. The diagnostic replay uses a separate full-depth scan, retains blocks 0, 8 and 17 (L1, L9 and L18), and takes the saved visibility masks as inputs. Final actions of the policy's own run are checked against the reference baseline before H is adopted; diagnostic readouts and velocities are checked exactly against in-graph controls.

The replay shows how the allocation P becomes the projected output W. It recomputes the prefix; normalized suffix states give queries, keys and values, with rotary embeddings on queries and keys and head-dimension scaling of queries. Cached prefix keys and values are concatenated with the suffix's, and softmax spans all visible keys before value aggregation. $W$ is the output projection before the adaptive attention gate and residual addition; the feed-forward sublayer and its gated residual then give post-block H. Prefix V stays fixed within a query; suffix V changes with the generation state. Masked attention logits are accumulated at higher precision and cast to the model precision before value aggregation.
