\subsection{Influence of individual Camera tasks}
\label{sec:auc-influence}
The Camera attention-output AUROC is not carried by a single task: it stays above one half when any one task is removed (Table~\ref{tab:auc-influence}). Before deletion it is $0.842$ for the L18 attention output at the first generation step, on ten eligible tasks with 15 lost successes and 23 joint successes.

\paragraph{Method.}
Each same-task SF--SS comparison keeps its original setting and noise-draw weights, and eligible tasks are weighted equally. Each eligible task is removed once, and the table gives the average over the remaining tasks. The deletion rule was fixed before these calculations but after the association had been seen, so this is a post hoc influence analysis.
