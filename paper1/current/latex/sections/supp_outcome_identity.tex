With $\chi$ the probability of a changed outcome and $\Delta\mathrm{SR}$ the success change, lost and gained successes, defined below, give the paired identities
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\chi&=\Pr(\mathrm{lost})+\Pr(\mathrm{gained}),\\
\Delta\mathrm{SR}&=\Pr(\mathrm{gained})-\Pr(\mathrm{lost}).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:outcome}
\end{equation}
